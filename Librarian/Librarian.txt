Librarian
--------- Created by by Nyerguds (25/01/2019)

Librarian is a simple game archive management tool.

It was originally created for the Mythos LIB archives, but support for more
archives (including Westwood PAK, and maybe even MIX) will be added eventually.

WARNING: At this moment, the tool does not have ANY error feedback, and will
always fail silently! I'll get on that later.

Version history:
---------------

v1.0.0: (25/01/2019 12:05)
